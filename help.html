Frequently Asked Questions

How does All In One Shopping Hub compare prices? +
Our backend queries top e-commerce platforms (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Croma, etc.) simultaneously, categorizes the search term, and ranks deals from lowest to highest so you can buy directly from the store offering the best price.
How does the Camera / Image Search feature work? +
You can upload an existing photo from your gallery or take a live snap using your camera. Our AI vision system analyzes the image features (e.g. green shirt, lipstick shade) and displays visual product matches across all eligible store cards.
Why don't all platforms show up for every search? +
We enforce strict category filtering for accuracy! For instance, cosmetics stores like Nykaa or Purplle won't show up for laptop queries, and electronic stores like Croma won't show up for fashion searches.
Do I complete my payment on All In One Shopping Hub? +
No. When you click "Explore" or "Buy From Best Store", we redirect you safely directly to the official platform's product page (e.g. Amazon or Flipkart) where you can complete your purchase securely.
Is my search history and profile data safe? +
Yes, completely! Your search history is stored securely to give you quick access to recent searches. You can clear your data at any time by clicking "Logout & Delete Data" from your profile dropdown menu.
